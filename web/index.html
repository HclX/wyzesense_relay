Hands-free provisioning station (2-pin cJTAG & 4-pad SBL)

  1. Identify. Hold a sensor on the probes; the station finds its pins with the chosen transport (cJTAG on M/C, polarity found; SBL: which of four unmarked pads are R, T and the backdoor; Auto: cJTAG, then SBL), confirms them, and lets it go. Nothing is read but what identifies it, nothing written.
  2. Dump or Flash. Put a sensor back on the same pads; only the identified pins are used.

Page 31 (CCFG + factory MAC) is never erased or written.

CCFG / Chip

Four wires: the sensor's + and − on clips (its battery out), and two hand probes, used for every mode. cJTAG: the probes on M and C. Bootloader: on its T and R pads (found which is which). Then W/S: probe 1 on T (or R), probe 2 on one of the other two pads: the station powers the sensor up with that pad released and then held low; if holding it kept the sensor from starting, it is S (reset), otherwise W.

Hands-free station flash (pages 0–29)

Stage a CC1310 binary (up to 120 KB, pages 0–29) and arm hands-free flashing over the selected transport on the identified pins (Identify first). Requires a completed and downloaded stock flash dump for that sensor MAC first.

Relay firmware (this relay)

The relay's build, firmware.bin. It carries the gateway firmware for the contact board: after its restart the relay checks the contact and updates it unless startup auto-sync is paused (paired sensors stay).
